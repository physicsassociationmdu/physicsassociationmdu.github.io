About the Association

Learning • Exploring • Discovering • Inspiring

Where Curiosity Meets the Universe

The Physics Association of Madhabdev University is an academic and intellectual initiative of the fraternity of faculty members and students of the Department of Physics.

The Association aims to create an engaging platform for scientific discussion, learning, research, innovation and the exploration of the fundamental laws governing nature and the universe.

From the quantum world to black holes, from classical mechanics to cosmology, we believe that physics provides a unique language through which we can understand reality.

Our Universe is a Laboratory

Question the known. Explore the unknown. Discover the laws that shape the cosmos.
